Who We Are

Travel World is a travel website created to help people discover amazing destinations and plan unforgettable journeys.

Our goal is to make travel planning simple by providing information about destinations, travel packages and exciting experiences.

This website is also designed as a beginner-friendly DevOps project using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Docker, Jenkins and AWS.
